Activity

Plug the robot into the PC running SCARA Twin Control, pick its port and connect. Then press Home switches (or, without limit switches, put the arm in the ready pose and press Zero here).

Connect to the PC

On the PC run scara_twin_server.py (MuJoCo, Gazebo or real robot). It prints the address to type here. Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.

How to use the app

Connect

On the PC start scara_twin_server.py (MuJoCo, Gazebo or the real robot). It prints an address like 192.168.1.50 - tap the status pill at the top, type it, port 9090, Connect. Same Wi-Fi. No PC? Try offline runs the robot, the table and the conveyor cell on the phone.

What you control

Simulation moves the simulated arm, Real robot the real one, Twin both (the real robot copies the simulation). The big red STOP freezes everything; tap it again to release.

Manual tab

Joysticks / Sliders at the top: sliders set each joint and the gripper exactly.

Joints: left stick = column (left/right) and height (up/down), right stick = elbow and wrist. XY + height: left stick moves the gripper over the table in straight lines (up on the stick = away from you in the 3-D view), right stick = height (up/down) and wrist turn (left/right). Tilt drives with the phone's tilt instead of the left stick.

To pick: open the gripper, bring it over the puck, lower it. When it is within about 4 cm the gripper centres itself (pick assist). Go all the way down, press Close, lift. Move, lower, Open. The gripper slider closes it part way.

Safe is slow with all guards, Normal faster, Off no limits. Tap to move a puck: tap a puck in the 3-D view, then tap where it should go - the robot does it.

Auto tab

Sorting: every colour goes into its own bin. Command: one bin, the robot picks only the colour you choose and keeps picking it until you choose another one. Any colour picks everything, Stop picking lets everything pass. Build a sequence: tap colours in order, then Run - one of each, in that order. Or type / dictate in the box: "red then blue", "3 green", "any", "stop", "start". Start / Stop run the conveyor.

Voice

Tap the microphone at the top (or Speak in the Auto tab) and say what you want: "red", "red then blue", "3 green", "any colour", "stop picking", "start", "stop", "open", "close", "home", "sorting", "command mode", "table", "emergency stop", "resume". On the phone it uses the phone's own speech recogniser (offline if the phone has offline speech installed); in a browser it needs Chrome.

Gesture control

Gesture (under the 3-D view, or in the Manual tab) opens the camera in its own place: above the robot on a phone, at the top of the right-hand column on a tablet or PC, so the robot view keeps its full size. Drag the bar under the picture to make it taller or shorter; Window makes it a floating window (drag the title, resize from the corner), Small a corner preview (tap it to dock it again), ✕ closes it. Hold your open palm in the yellow square for two seconds. Then your hand is the left joystick: keep it in the inner circle to stand still, move it to move the arm (full speed at the outer circle). Make a fist to close the gripper, open your hand to open it. Plain background and even light help; Learn again (or double-tap the picture) if the light changes.

Gamepad / joystick

Plug a USB gamepad into the PC, or pair a Bluetooth one (or plug it in with an OTG cable) on the phone - Zebronics, Xbox, PlayStation or any other. Press a button once so the browser sees it. Left stick = left joystick, right stick = right joystick (d-pad works too). A / cross close the gripper, B / circle open it, X / square home, Y / triangle save the pose, LB / RB slower / faster, Start = emergency stop (again to release), Select = belt on / off.

Teach tab

Move the arm, Save this pose, repeat, then Play (or Loop). Go / Delete per pose. Exact position sliders put each joint exactly where you want it.

Robot tab

Pick the robot's port on the PC, Connect robot (or Software robot to try), then Home switches. Motors free / on, and in camera-cell mode the pose buttons for the real conveyor cell.

FK / IK tab

Live forward kinematics: where the gripper is (X, Y, height, yaw), its reach, the elbow side and how far it is from the straight-arm singularity. Inverse kinematics: type a point (or Tap the table), see both elbow solutions with their joint limits, press Go. Robot frame = origin on the column axis. Below: the motion graph of the last 10 s, Record / Save CSV for your report, and the gripper's path.

View

Drag to turn, pinch (or mouse wheel) to zoom. Front / Top / Follow (the camera follows the gripper). The small window is the arm camera on the forearm - pinch it (or drag its corner with the mouse) to make it smaller, double-tap for full size. The sun / moon button switches dark and light (key T).

Keyboard (computer or phone)

Works with the PC keyboard and with a USB / Bluetooth keyboard plugged into the phone. A/D or ←/→ column, W/S or ↑/↓ height (in XY + height mode: move over the table), J/L elbow, I/K wrist, G close, O open, H home, P save pose, + / - speed, X joints / XY, V voice, T dark / light, Space stop / release. The keys are shown in the Manual tab as soon as you press one.
